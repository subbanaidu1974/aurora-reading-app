About Aurora

Aurora is built with one clear belief: accessibility should never be an afterthought.

For millions of people with dyslexia, navigating the web can be exhausting—dense text, poor contrast, cluttered layouts, and unreadable fonts turn simple tasks into barriers. Aurora exists to change that.

We design and build digital experiences that are calm, clear, and inclusive by default. Every decision—layout, typography, spacing, color, and interaction—is intentionally crafted to reduce cognitive load and improve readability. The result is a platform that feels natural, supportive, and empowering for people with dyslexia, without compromising on design or performance.

Aurora is not just about compliance or checklists. It's about human-centered design. We focus on how real people read, process information, and interact with content in the real world.

What We Stand For

  • Clarity over complexity – Simple language, clean layouts, and distraction-free design.
  • Accessibility by design – Dyslexia-friendly principles are embedded from the start.
  • Inclusive innovation – Technology should adapt to people, not the other way around.
  • Respect and dignity – Everyone deserves a web experience that works for them.

Our Mission

To create digital spaces where people with dyslexia can read, learn, and engage with confidence—without friction, frustration, or compromise.

Our Vision

A future where the web is naturally inclusive, and accessibility is a standard—not a special feature.

Aurora is a step toward that future.
